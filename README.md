Vice City Cover Maker turns your photo and a caption into a multi-panel poster cover in the style of a Vice City sunset or neon night, with bold gradient lettering, and exports it as a PNG in square, portrait or wide post sizes. Pick a layout, drop in one photo per panel or one photo across all of them, drag to crop, choose a colour grade, and export in one click — everything runs offline in the browser and your photos never leave your machine.

Three layouts (single panel, 3-panel poster, 2 x 2 grid), three colour grades (Vice City sunset, neon night, noir), per-panel crop and zoom, and one-click PNG export at 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 and 1920 x 1080. Three original Vice City-style sample photos ship with the app so the first cover is one click away.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
